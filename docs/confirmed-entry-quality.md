# Confirmed entry quality and outcome follow-up

Authorized scope: Colmex and Binance. The implementation replaces immediate intrabar Spring entries with a subsequent closed five-minute reclaim above first support. The independent early-wave route now also requires a closed breakout. Both routes share causal trend/volume checks and retain distinct identities. RSI 0–40 remains a live check for Spring; early-wave still has no RSI ceiling. No short executor is introduced.

Initial parameters (hypotheses to monitor, not optimized or proven profitable):

Binance stop update, 29 October 2026: the owner's Settings value is now the only source of the initial stop-loss percentage, for new and existing managed positions. Binance does not widen it using ATR/structure or freeze a separate per-position override. The volatility-based stop and frozen-entry rules below now apply to Colmex only. See [Binance settings-only stop](binance-settings-stop.md).

- Block a long only when price is below both SMA100/SMA200 and both averages decline over four closed five-minute bars. Require 204 valid bars. Stock sessions retain verified overnight gaps; Binance uses continuous five-minute data.
- Confirmation volume must be at least the mean of the preceding 20 closed bars, excluding the confirmation. Missing volume/history blocks entry.
- Confirmation expires after 15 minutes. Recheck fresh price, support/breakout, Spring RSI and live-bar invalidation immediately before submission. Colmex checks again after durable reservation, before the broker write.
- Colmex initial stop distance is the greater of the configured baseline, 1.5 × Wilder ATR14, and distance below the pattern low with a 0.1 ATR buffer. Round the percentage up to two decimals; reject a required distance over 3% instead of clipping it. ATR measures volatility, not direction ([Fidelity indicator guide](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/atr)). Binance uses the configured percentage exactly.
- Planned price risk is capped at the smaller of $8 and 1% of the prior allocation (or the configured stop percentage if smaller). Reduce quantity/notional as needed for the applicable stop. This is not a guarantee against gaps, slippage or commissions. Keep cash reserves, exchange lot/minimum rules, exposure limits and native brackets. New Colmex positions freeze their entry risk distance; Binance follows current Settings. Existing tighter stops and earned trailing protection are preserved.

Monitoring uses existing reads/cycles. Real closures are grouped by entry route and policy version. Colmex preserves provenance even for a position closed before its first poll; unverified fees remain null. Binance aggregates partial exits into a closed trade and excludes incomplete fee data from net results. Realized drawdown describes closed trades, not account equity including open positions.

A separate forward observation report compares legacy signals, closed-confirmation-only signals, confirmation plus trend, confirmation plus volume, and all three. It measures subsequent price change around 30 minutes, **not strategy profit**: it does not simulate fills, fees or stops. Unknown continuation is marked missing. Duplicate patterns are suppressed across restarts; observed keys expire after seven days and bounded buffers report dropped samples. Binance observations cross UTC midnight; stock observations do not cross dates. This begins with deployment and never rewrites prior history. All-filter live deployment is not a randomized experiment; observational comparisons cannot establish causal improvement or promise future returns.

Verification: all 852 tests passed locally. This includes the full automated suite plus offline broker-adapter tests for both final order paths, stale/invalid confirmation rejection, risk sizing and persistence, baseline separation, missing fees, replay consistency, and UI unknown-state handling. No live order is placed as a test.

Deployment: Netlify updates Colmex runtime and both frontends from main. Binance's separate VM requires `scripts/install-confirmed-quality.sh FULL_COMMIT_SHA` in `/opt/levi-binance` after fetching the commit. The installer stages and syntax-checks runtime files, backs up existing code, keeps credentials/settings/position files intact, restarts the existing service, checks the public policy version and rolls code back on failure. A published frontend alone does not establish that the VM was updated.
